      Know the terms

      Three things to understand before comparing offers

      Rate vs. APR

      Your interest rate sets your monthly payment. The APR adds certain fees, like origination charges and points, spread over the loan. Compare APRs between lenders.

      Discount points

      One point costs 1% of your loan amount and lowers your rate. It pays off only if you keep the loan long enough to recover the cost.

      Try the points calculator →

      Rate lock

      A rate lock holds your rate for a set period, often 30 to 60 days, so market changes don't affect you while your loan closes.
