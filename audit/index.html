Six ways to start, and no clear first step

Finding 3 · Upkeep

Some pages promise more than they hold

Blog

One post, dated January 1, 2023. It reads “Welcome to ! Please check back for future articles and information.”

Recommended Professionals

Seven categories, from contractors to insurance agents. Every one shows 0 entries.

Local Resources

Four categories. Three are empty, and one lists a single title company.

Finding 4 · Words

Copy that could belong to any lender, and one fact to fix

“What are you waiting for? Getting started is easy!”Home page banner, over a stock photo
“Zero Cost · Zero Obligation · Zero Hassle”Sidebar on several pages
“FREE advice … regarding any Mortgage and/or Real Estate related topics”Ask a Professional

Home Search, Home Values and Find A Realtor send visitors to real estate tools, which blurs what Reliant does. The same autumn-road photo heads every inner page.

Accuracy · FHA Loans page

“To be eligible for an FHA loan, the borrower must have a credit score of at least 500 and put down a minimum of 3.5% of the home's purchase price.”

The page says a 500 credit score qualifies with 3.5% down. Under HUD's rules, 3.5% down needs a score of 580 or higher. From 500 to 579, the minimum down payment is 10%.

Worth correcting now, whatever you decide about a redesign.

Finding 6 · Under the hood

Solid on accessibility, heavier than it needs to be

Google Lighthouse, phone test, middle of three runs.

  • 94 requests and about 1.1 MB to load the home page, including about 440 KB of scripts on every page.
  • The main content appears after about 6.0 seconds on Lighthouse's simulated phone.
  • 16 of 17 pages have no meta description, the summary Google often shows under your link.
  • Staff listings, forms, directories and the newsletter load in embedded frames after the page. On the staff page the layout jumps as they arrive (shift score 0.45; Google rates over 0.25 as poor).
  • Lighthouse flags third-party cookies, and the newsletter page throws a script error.

Decision 6 · A moment people remember

Sending the form feels like arriving home

  • Most sites answer a form with “Thank you for your submission.” This turns that moment into a small welcome people remember.
  • On the home page, visitors drag the model home to walk around it.
  • Still being smoothed. Your feedback was “getting better, but clunky and not smooth.” Next: load every view up front, blend views with motion, and move the walk to one graphics canvas.
  • Visitors who turn off motion on their device skip it entirely.

Decision 7 · Everyone, every screen

Built for phones first, and for every visitor

  • No sideways scrolling at phone width.
